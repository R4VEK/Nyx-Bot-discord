# Nyx — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Nyx**, a Discord bot, may process information when it is added to or used within a Discord server.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used and the configuration of a server, Nyx may process information such as:

* Discord user IDs.
* Discord server IDs.
* Discord channel IDs.
* Discord role IDs.
* Discord message content when required for a specific command or feature.
* Usernames, display names, and other information made available to the bot through Discord.
* Moderation-related information, such as warnings or moderation actions, if the corresponding features are enabled.
* Technical information required to operate and troubleshoot the bot.

Nyx should only process information necessary for the functionality being provided.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

* Provide Nyx's commands and features.
* Perform moderation actions requested by authorized users.
* Respond to commands and interactions.
* Maintain server-specific settings.
* Detect and troubleshoot errors.
* Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
* Improve the operation of the bot.

## 3. AI-Powered Features

Nyx provides AI-powered responses through an external artificial intelligence API provided by **NVIDIA**.

When a user interacts with Nyx's AI functionality, the content of the relevant Discord message may be sent to NVIDIA's API in order to generate a response.

Nyx may send the following information as part of an AI request:

* The content of the user's Discord message.
* The instructions used to define Nyx's behavior and personality.
* Information contained in the message that is necessary to generate the response.

Based on the functionality described in the current version of Nyx, the bot sends the current message to the AI service when:

* Nyx is directly mentioned.
* A user replies to a message from Nyx.
* The message contains the name "Nyx".

Nyx does not intentionally send unrelated Discord messages to the AI service through this functionality.

### 3.1 Conversation History

The current AI implementation sends the current user message together with Nyx's system instructions.

The code provided for the current implementation does **not** show persistent storage of the user's previous AI conversations, only if the user says to store the information or if the content is relevant to the AI.

Therefore, Nyx does not claim to provide persistent AI conversation memory unless such functionality is separately implemented.

### 3.2 Third-Party AI Provider

AI requests are processed through NVIDIA's API infrastructure.

NVIDIA may process information transmitted through its API according to its applicable terms, documentation, and privacy practices.

Users should avoid sending passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, private keys, or other highly sensitive information to Nyx's AI features.

### 3.3 AI Accuracy

AI-generated responses are generated automatically and may contain inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate information.

Nyx's AI responses should not be considered guaranteed factual information or professional advice.


## 4. Data Retention

The retention period depends on the type of information and the features implemented by Nyx.

Some information may only be processed temporarily, while other information may be retained when required to provide functionality such as server configuration or moderation records.

If a specific retention period applies to a particular feature, it should be documented separately.

## 5. Data Sharing

Nyx does not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate specific Nyx features, such as:

* Discord.
* AI/API providers used by Nyx.
* Hosting or infrastructure providers.

Those providers may process information according to their own policies and terms.

## 6. Discord

Nyx operates through Discord's platform and may receive information made available through Discord's APIs.

Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own policies and terms.

## 7. Server Administrators

Server owners and administrators control many aspects of how Nyx is used in their server.

They may configure commands, permissions, moderation features, and other functionality available to them.

Server administrators are responsible for ensuring that their use of Nyx is appropriate for their community.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information processed by Nyx.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

API keys, authentication tokens, and other credentials should never be publicly shared or committed to a public repository.

## 9. Data Requests

If you have a question about information processed by Nyx or want to request information concerning your data, contact the Nyx owner through the official support/contact method associated with the bot.

Requests may require sufficient information to identify the relevant Discord account or server.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Nyx is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the service.

Discord users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nyx's functionality, data processing, or legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this document indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

## 12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Nyx owner through the official support/contact method associated with the bot.
